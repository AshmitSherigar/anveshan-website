Getting Started with CNNs

AS By Anish S · Jul 15, 2025

Convolutional Neural Networks, or CNNs, are one of the most important ideas behind modern computer vision.

Instead of looking at every pixel independently, a CNN learns small visual patterns such as edges, shapes, and textures. It then combines those patterns layer by layer to understand more complex objects.

How does a CNN see?

The first layers usually detect simple details. Deeper layers combine those details into meaningful features, allowing the network to recognize things like faces, vehicles, or handwritten numbers.

This is what makes CNNs useful for image classification, object detection, medical imaging, and many other visual tasks.

The simple idea

A CNN does not understand an image the way a person does. It learns a hierarchy of patterns from examples. With enough useful training data, those patterns become a powerful way to turn pixels into predictions.
